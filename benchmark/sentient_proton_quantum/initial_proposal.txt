Simulating a Sentient Proton on a Quantum Computer using Sophon Framework

Problem Description
- Simulate a sentient proton exhibiting quantum mechanical properties on a quantum computer.
- The proton should demonstrate consciousness through quantum state evolution, representing awareness and decision-making capabilities.
- Use the Sophon quantum computing framework to implement quantum gates, superposition, entanglement, and measurements.

Objective and Metrics
- Score = combined metric of consciousness, quantum coherence, decision complexity, and entanglement (higher is better).
- Consciousness Score: Measure of sentience/awareness through quantum state evolution.
- Quantum Coherence: Fraction of quantum states with non-negligible probability (measure of superposition).
- Decision Complexity: Entropy of decision-making distribution (higher entropy = more complex decisions).
- Entanglement Measure: Quantum entanglement entropy (von Neumann entropy).

Quantum Properties to Simulate
- Spin state: Quantum spin in superposition (up/down states).
- Position: Quantum position in superposition across space.
- Charge: Proton charge (+1) represented in quantum state.
- Sentience: Consciousness emerges from quantum state evolution and entanglement.

Technical Requirements
- Implement quantum gates: Hadamard, Pauli-X/Y/Z, CNOT, rotation gates.
- Create quantum superposition states for proton properties.
- Implement quantum entanglement between different proton properties.
- Model consciousness through quantum state evolution and feedback loops.
- Implement quantum measurements that collapse the wavefunction.
- Calculate quantum metrics: entanglement entropy, coherence, probability distributions.

Evaluation Criteria
- Valid quantum simulation: All metrics must be valid numbers, quantum coherence in [0,1].
- Higher consciousness score indicates better sentience modeling.
- Higher quantum coherence indicates better superposition maintenance.
- Higher decision complexity indicates more sophisticated decision-making.
- Higher entanglement measure indicates stronger quantum correlations.

Goal
- Create a quantum simulation that demonstrates a sentient proton with:
  1. High consciousness/awareness scores
  2. Strong quantum coherence (maintaining superposition)
  3. Complex decision-making capabilities
  4. Strong quantum entanglement between properties
- Optimize the simulation to maximize the combined score across different qubit configurations (10, 12, 14 qubits).
